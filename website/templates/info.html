Info
This website is made for the Databases and Information Systems course (DIS) block 4 2023.
The purpose of this site is to inspect geochemical data. You can add .csv-files under uploads, which are saved in a database as individual tables. You can inspect your tables and generate Harker diagrams on the inspect table page.
The app only saves the major element data, but will be extended with more plotting and inspection options during the summer holidays of 2023. :) By group 111.
